Tiny-GenImage Benchmark Properties

The GenImage benchmark was introduced to address the limitation of existing detectors that fail to generalize across generators. Tiny-GenImage subsets 5,000 balanced images per generator:

  • Diverse Generators: Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, GLIDE, BigGAN, ADM, VQDM, Wukong.
  • Multi-Domain Categories: Preserves ImageNet classes (animals, nature, vehicles, architecture, objects).
  • Cross-Generator Robustness: Prevents overfitting to a single model's watermarks or specific prompt patterns.

ConvNeXt-Tiny Architectural Advantages

Why ConvNeXt-Tiny outperformed traditional CNNs and EfficientNet on this task:

  • 7x7 Depthwise Convolutions: Large receptive fields capture macro visual layout and subtle non-local diffusion coherence similar to ViTs while retaining convolution efficiency.
  • Inverted Bottleneck Design: 1x1 conv expansion followed by GELU and LayerNorm regularizes high-frequency noise representation.
  • Colab Robustness Training: Trained with Random JPEG Compression (quality 40-95) and Label Smoothing (0.1) yielding 98.86% test accuracy and 99.90% ROC-AUC.
